# logo-reveal

Opener. Black and ivory fluted panels slide in from top and bottom and meet
at the logo's own black | ivory seam; the real panel logo is revealed flute by
flute; the tagline types on in Light 300 with a bronze rule underneath.

**Length:** 3 s (90 frames @30). Don't shorten below 2.5 s.

| Prop | Type | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tagline` | string | `WE FIT OUT YOUR SPACE` | Empty string hides it. Keep the official wording. |
| `background` | `split` \| `ink` \| `ivory` | `split` | `split` is the signature; solid for quieter edits. |

Timeline (frames @30, vertical): 0–18 panels · 14–49 logo wipe · 41–62 tagline · 62–90 hold.

**Do** use it once, first. **Don't** put text or other elements over it, or
re-time it to music by squashing the panel slide.
